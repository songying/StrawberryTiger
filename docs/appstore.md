## Detailed Submission Tutorial

This section provides a complete, step-by-step guide to submitting Strawberry Tiger to the Apple App Store. Follow each step carefully.

### Step 1: Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have the following:

1. **Apple Developer Account**
   - You need an Apple Developer Program membership ($99/year).
   - Sign up at [https://developer.apple.com/programs/](https://developer.apple.com/programs/).
   - Enrollment can take 24-48 hours to process.
   - You must use a real identity; Apple verifies your information.

2. **Xcode**
   - Install the latest version of Xcode from the Mac App Store.
   - Make sure Xcode command-line tools are installed: open Terminal and run `xcode-select --install`.

3. **Apple ID signed into Xcode**
   - Open Xcode > Settings (Cmd+,) > Accounts tab.
   - Click the "+" button and sign in with your Apple Developer account.

4. **Your project builds and runs correctly**
   - Open the project in Xcode and verify it builds without errors.
   - Test on a physical device if possible (not just the simulator).

5. **Screenshots ready**
   - Have all screenshot files prepared in the sizes listed above.

6. **Privacy policy page published**
   - Host the privacy policy at `https://alvin-game.github.io/StrawberryTiger/privacy` before submission.

---

### Step 2: Create an App ID in Apple Developer Portal

An App ID uniquely identifies your app. You may have already created one; if so, skip to Step 3.

1. Go to [https://developer.apple.com/account/resources/identifiers/list](https://developer.apple.com/account/resources/identifiers/list).
2. Click the **"+"** button to register a new identifier.
3. Select **"App IDs"** and click **Continue**.
4. Select **"App"** (not App Clip) and click **Continue**.
5. Fill in:
   - **Description:** `Strawberry Tiger`
   - **Bundle ID:** Select **"Explicit"** and enter `com.strawberrytiger.game`
6. Under **Capabilities**, you do not need to enable anything special for this game. Leave defaults.
7. Click **Continue**, review the information, then click **Register**.

---

### Step 3: Create the App in App Store Connect

App Store Connect is where you manage your app listing, builds, and submissions.

1. Go to [https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) and sign in.
2. Click **"Apps"** in the top navigation.
3. Click the **"+"** button in the top-left corner and select **"New App"**.
4. Fill in the form:
   - **Platforms:** Check **iOS**.
   - **Name:** `Strawberry Tiger`
   - **Primary Language:** English (U.S.)
   - **Bundle ID:** Select `com.strawberrytiger.game` from the dropdown. If it does not appear, go back to Step 2 and verify the App ID was registered.
   - **SKU:** Enter `strawberrytiger001` (this is an internal identifier and is not shown publicly).
   - **User Access:** Full Access (or limit to specific users if desired).
5. Click **Create**.

You will be taken to the app's page in App Store Connect.

### Step 5: Configure Xcode Project for Release

Before archiving, verify your Xcode project settings.

1. Open your Xcode project (`.xcodeproj` or `.xcworkspace`).
2. Select the **project** in the navigator (the blue icon at the top).
3. Select your **target** (the app target, not test targets).
4. Go to the **General** tab:
   - **Display Name:** `Strawberry Tiger`
   - **Bundle Identifier:** `com.strawberrytiger.game`
   - **Version:** `1.0` (or `1.0.0`)
   - **Build:** `1` (increment this for each upload)
   - **Deployment Target:** Set to the minimum iOS version you support (e.g., `15.0`).
   - **Device Orientation:** Ensure only **Landscape Left** and **Landscape Right** are checked. Uncheck Portrait and Upside Down.
   - **Devices:** iPhone and iPad (Universal).

5. Go to the **Signing & Capabilities** tab:
   - **Team:** Select your Apple Developer team.
   - **Signing Certificate:** Ensure "Automatically manage signing" is checked. Xcode will create the necessary provisioning profiles.
   - If you see any errors here, make sure your Apple Developer account is properly set up in Xcode > Settings > Accounts.

6. Go to the **Build Settings** tab:
   - Search for **"Code Signing Identity"**: Should be set to "Apple Distribution" for Release.
   - Search for **"Debug Information Format"**: Set to "DWARF with dSYM File" for Release (helps with crash reports).

7. **Test the Release build:** Select a physical device or "Any iOS Device (arm64)" as the build target, then press Cmd+B to build. Fix any errors.

### Step 6: Archive and Upload the Build

#### 6a. Create an Archive

1. In Xcode, select the build destination as **"Any iOS Device (arm64)"** (not a simulator).
2. From the menu bar, go to **Product > Archive**.
3. Xcode will build and archive your app. This may take a few minutes.
4. When complete, the **Organizer** window will open showing your archive.

If the Organizer does not open automatically, go to **Window > Organizer**.

#### 6b. Upload to App Store Connect

**Option A: Upload via Xcode (Recommended)**

1. In the Organizer, select your archive and click **"Distribute App"**.
2. Select **"App Store Connect"** and click **Next**.
3. Select **"Upload"** (not "Export") and click **Next**.
4. Review the distribution options:
   - **Strip Swift symbols:** Yes (reduces app size).
   - **Upload your app's symbols:** Yes (enables Apple to symbolicate crash reports).
   - **Manage Version and Build Number:** Yes.
5. Click **Next**. Xcode will check your app for issues.
6. If there are no errors, select the **distribution signing certificate and provisioning profile** (usually auto-selected if automatic signing is on).
7. Click **Upload**.
8. Wait for the upload to complete. This may take several minutes depending on your internet speed.
9. You will see a success message when the upload is complete.

**Option B: Upload via Transporter**

1. Download **Transporter** from the Mac App Store (free Apple app).
2. In the Xcode Organizer, select your archive and click **"Distribute App"**.
3. Choose **"Export"** instead of "Upload."
4. Follow the prompts to export an `.ipa` file to your disk.
5. Open Transporter, sign in with your Apple Developer account.
6. Drag and drop the `.ipa` file into Transporter.
7. Click **Deliver** to upload.

#### 6c. Wait for Processing

After uploading, Apple will process your build. This typically takes 5-30 minutes. You will receive an email when processing is complete. If there are issues (such as missing icons or invalid entitlements), you will receive an email detailing the problems.

### Step 9: Submit for Review

1. In App Store Connect, on your version page, click **"Add for Review"** (button in the top-right area).
2. You will see a summary of what you are submitting. Review it carefully.
3. Click **"Submit to App Review"**.
4. The status will change to **"Waiting for Review"**.

#### What Happens Next

- **Waiting for Review:** Your app is in the queue. Typical wait time is 24-48 hours, but it can vary.
- **In Review:** An Apple reviewer is actively examining your app. This usually takes a few hours.
- **Accepted / Ready for Sale:** Your app has been approved. If you selected automatic release, it will appear on the App Store shortly. If manual, go to App Store Connect and click "Release This Version."
- **Rejected:** See Step 10 below.

You can check the status at any time in App Store Connect. Apple also sends email notifications for status changes.

### Step 10: Common Rejection Reasons and How to Avoid Them

Here are the most common reasons apps get rejected and how to ensure Strawberry Tiger passes review.

#### 1. Crashes and Bugs
**The Problem:** The app crashes during review, freezes, or has obvious bugs.
**How to Avoid:**
- Test thoroughly on multiple devices and iOS versions.
- Test on a physical device, not just the simulator.
- Pay special attention to different screen sizes (iPhone SE, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPad).
- Test the full gameplay loop: menu > play > collect strawberries > hit a rock > game over > replay.

#### 2. Broken Links
**The Problem:** The Support URL or Privacy Policy URL leads to a 404 or blank page.
**How to Avoid:**
- Publish your GitHub Pages site **before** submitting.
- Test both URLs in a browser to confirm they load correctly.
- Wait a few minutes after deploying GitHub Pages, as it can take time to propagate.

#### 3. Missing or Incorrect Screenshots
**The Problem:** Screenshots do not match the actual app, are the wrong size, or show non-Apple devices.
**How to Avoid:**
- Take screenshots from the actual game running on the correct device sizes.
- Ensure screenshots are the exact pixel dimensions required.
- Do not add fake device frames or other elements that misrepresent the experience.

#### 4. Incomplete Metadata
**The Problem:** Missing description, missing screenshots for required device sizes, or placeholder text left in fields.
**How to Avoid:**
- Use the checklist in Step 8 above.
- Make sure there is no "lorem ipsum" or "[TODO]" text anywhere.

#### 5. Insufficient Content / Minimum Functionality
**The Problem:** Apple considers the app too simple or not useful enough. This is sometimes cited as Guideline 4.2.
**How to Avoid:**
- Strawberry Tiger has a complete gameplay loop (menu, game, scoring, game over, restart), which should be sufficient.
- In the review notes, clearly explain the gameplay and that it is a complete, intentional game experience.
- Having polished graphics, sound, and a scoring system demonstrates sufficient functionality.

#### 6. Privacy Policy Issues
**The Problem:** No privacy policy provided, or the policy does not match the app's data practices.
**How to Avoid:**
- Always include a privacy policy URL, even if the app collects no data.
- Make sure the policy is accurate (since we collect no data, state that clearly).

#### 7. Guideline 4.3 - Spam / Copycat
**The Problem:** Apple may flag apps that are too similar to existing apps on the store.
**How to Avoid:**
- Strawberry Tiger has a unique visual style (procedurally drawn graphics) which differentiates it.
- The game's name and branding are original.
- If Apple raises this concern, explain in your response what makes the game unique.

#### 8. App Icon Issues
**The Problem:** The app icon is missing required sizes or does not match Apple's specifications.
**How to Avoid:**
- In Xcode, open the Asset Catalog (`Assets.xcassets`) and ensure the `AppIcon` image set has all required sizes filled in.
- The icon must be 1024x1024 pixels for the App Store.
- The icon must not have rounded corners (Apple applies them automatically).
- The icon must not have any transparency.

#### 9. Performance and Device Compatibility
**The Problem:** The app does not run well on all supported devices.
**How to Avoid:**
- Test on the oldest device you intend to support.
- Make sure the game runs at a smooth frame rate on older devices.
- Ensure the landscape orientation works correctly and there are no layout issues on any screen size.

#### 10. Responding to a Rejection
If your app is rejected:
1. Read the rejection message carefully in App Store Connect (under "Resolution Center").
2. Fix the cited issue.
3. If you believe the rejection is a mistake, you can reply in the Resolution Center to explain your case.
4. After fixing issues, increment the build number in Xcode, create a new archive, upload it, select the new build, and submit again.
5. Re-submissions typically get reviewed faster than initial submissions.

## Additional Tips

### Expedited Review
If you need your app reviewed urgently (for example, a critical bug fix), you can request an expedited review at [https://developer.apple.com/contact/app-store/?topic=expedite](https://developer.apple.com/contact/app-store/?topic=expedite). This is generally reserved for critical issues and should not be used for initial submissions.

### App Store Optimization (ASO)
- The app name, subtitle, and keywords are the primary factors in App Store search ranking.
- The first few lines of your description are visible without tapping "more," so make them compelling.
- Update your promotional text periodically (it does not require a new review) to keep the listing fresh.

### After Approval
- Monitor crash reports in App Store Connect and Xcode Organizer.
- Read user reviews and respond to feedback.
- Plan updates with new features, bug fixes, or seasonal content to keep the game fresh.
- Consider adding a Game Center leaderboard in a future update for added engagement.

### Updating the App
To release an update:
1. Increment the version number (e.g., 1.0 to 1.1) and build number in Xcode.
2. Archive and upload the new build.
3. In App Store Connect, create a new version, fill in the "What's New" field, select the new build, and submit for review.
