Privacy Policy

Strawberry Tiger — Last updated: March 28, 2026

Strawberry Tiger does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information.

Data Collection

Strawberry Tiger collects absolutely no data from its users. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage data or analytics are gathered
  • No device identifiers are accessed
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used
  • No location data is collected

Network Access

Strawberry Tiger operates entirely offline. The game makes no network requests of any kind. No data ever leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Strawberry Tiger does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect data. There are:

  • No advertising frameworks
  • No analytics services
  • No crash reporting tools
  • No social media integrations

In-App Purchases

Strawberry Tiger contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or any form of monetization.

Children's Privacy

Since Strawberry Tiger collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages. The game complies with COPPA, GDPR, and all other applicable privacy regulations.

Local Storage

The only data stored locally on your device is your high score, which is saved using the platform's standard preferences system. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to any external service.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Given the nature of the app, significant changes are unlikely.
